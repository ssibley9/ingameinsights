01

Playbook Management

Draw it once. Use it all week, and on game day.

  • Chart your plays. Draw offensive plays with routes and motion, and defensive calls with alignments and zone responsibilities. Formations, personnel groups, and special teams included.
  • Offense and defense, side by side. Both sides of the ball in one team playbook, organized by categories you choose.
  • Build call sheets and wristbands. Assemble game-day call sheets and wristband panels using your own play-calling terminology.
  • Print with play diagrams. One-page call sheets, play sheets, and wristband cards sized to real wristband windows, with every play drawn.
  • Sideline whiteboard. Start from any formation or play, sketch the adjustment in real time, and save it as a new play.
02

Record Stats

Fast enough for the sideline. Deep enough for the film room.

  • Touch-first live input. Tap players and spots on the field to log each snap in seconds. Score, clock, down, and distance keep themselves.
  • Your playbook drives the stats. Tag the call on every snap and see play-call effectiveness in real time, broken down by call, situation, and drive. This is the part no other app can do.
  • Share live to other iPads. Assistants follow score, situation, and every stat tab on a bench iPad as it happens, without touching the inputs.
  • Game reports. HTML game reports and CSV exports, plus drive charts that draw every possession on a field.
  • Full-season totals. Records, team totals, and player lines roll up automatically across the season.
03

Scouting

Know what's coming before they line up.

  • Chart opponent film. Log each snap's formation, play, defensive front, coverage, and result, linked to its spot in the film.
  • Find their tendencies. Scouting reports break down what they do by down, distance, field zone, formation, and hash — ready to print and hand out.
  • Diagram their plays. Draw the opponent's plays on the same canvas as your playbook, so your scout team simulates them accurately.
  • Self-scout automatically. Every game you track feeds the same tendency reports for your own team. Spot your own patterns before opponents do — with no extra charting.
  • Prepare both sides. Chart both teams in one game and scout their offense and defense from the same film.
