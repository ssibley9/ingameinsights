01

Ball tracking

Track the ball the way the game is actually played: tap who has it, then tap who they pass to. The grids change role with the game state, so what is on screen is always what can happen next.

  • Complete pass chains. Who passed to whom, completions, incompletions, interceptions and throwaways.
  • The assist is pre-selected from the last pass in the chain when a goal goes in.
  • The grids change with possession. Handler and Receiver when you have it, Knockdown when they do, Groundball + Possession when it is loose.
  • Opponent passing tracked as complete, loose or turnover, without needing their jersey numbers.
02

Automation, so you tap less

Most of what follows an event is predictable. The app does that part itself.

  • Possession updates itself after turnovers, takeaways, interceptions, saves and groundballs.
  • The field zone flips automatically when possession changes.
  • Knockdown to takeaway. If your knockdown leads to your team getting the ball, that player is credited with the takeaway. It works in the other direction too.
  • Save outcome is a choice. The goalie controls it and possession flips, or it is a live rebound and the ball stays loose.
  • After a goal, the app jumps straight to the faceoff player picker.
  • Picking a faceoff player subs them on automatically.
  • A penalty sets man-up or man-down without you telling it.
  • A failed clear or a successful ride prompts a one-tap possession flip.
03

Shot charting

Two taps: where it came from on the field, and where it went on the goal.

  • Tap the field for the exact spot the shot came from.
  • Tap the goal for where it went — including miss high, miss wide left, miss wide right and blocked.
  • Results. Goal, saved, missed, blocked.
  • Every shot plotted as a dot and rolled into zone heat maps.
  • Per-player shot zones — where each player shoots from, and where on the goal they put it.
04

Voice commands

Run the clock, record stats and track passes without touching the screen.

  • Examples. “Start clock”, “groundball 23”, “turnover”, “faceoff… won”, “clear… success”, “sub 14”, “starters”.
  • Shots by voice. Field zone (“in left”), goal spot (“top right”), then “goal” or “saved”.
  • In ball tracking, say jersey numbers to log passes, plus “incomplete” or “intercepted”.
  • Understands player names, “goalie”, “opponent”, and team jersey colors — “white ball”.
  • Possession-aware. A bare “turnover” or “takeaway” knows which team it belongs to.
  • Custom alias dictionary for words it keeps mishearing, and “reset” to clear a misheard phrase.
  • Handles 0 and 00.
05

Lines and substitutions

  • Saved lines. Starters, man-up, man-down, faceoff, attack, three midfield sets and three defensive sets.
  • Apply a line with one tap, or by voice.
  • Sub popup with an on-field count badge.
  • Mark players unavailable for a given game.
  • One goalie on the field is enforced.
  • Minutes and quarters played tracked automatically from subs and the clock.
06

Penalty timers

Penalties are their own small job during a game. The app does that job.

  • Floating, draggable countdown timers that start and stop with the game clock.
  • Release a penalty early, for instance after a man-up goal.
  • Alerts when a penalty expires.
  • Sync to the official clock when entering a penalty.
  • Opponent jersey numbers and locked, non-releasable penalties.
07

Context on every play

A stat with no situation attached cannot tell you much afterwards. Every event carries its circumstances.

  • Stamped on every event. Quarter, game clock, running score.
  • Field position. Offensive, midfield, defensive, the box, behind the goal.
  • Situation. Settled, unsettled, fast break, man-up, man-down, clear, ride.
  • Opponent faceoff specialists tracked by jersey number.
08

Corrections

  • Undo toast on every stat.
  • The event log is editable. Delete an event and its stats reverse.
  • Reassign a stat to a different player.
  • Manual team and player adjustments, each logged in the event history.
09

Game formats and clock

  • Levels. Varsity, JV, freshman and rec.
  • Quarters or halves, custom period length, and overtime.
  • Running-clock mode for youth and rec games.
  • Mark a game final, or undo it.
10

Every stat captured

Team, both sides

  • Goals, assists, shots, shots on goal, shooting percentage
  • Groundballs, turnovers forced and unforced, takeaways, knockdowns, blocked shots
  • Faceoffs taken and won, faceoff percentage
  • Clear attempts, successes and percentage — rides tracked as the opponent’s clears
  • Man-up opportunities, goals and percentage, plus man-down defense
  • Penalties and penalty minutes
  • Saves and timeouts remaining
  • Possessions and cleared possessions
  • Possession time by offensive, midfield and defensive zone, and by quarter
  • Pass attempts, completions and percentage in ball tracking mode

Individual players

  • Goals, assists, points, shots, shots on goal, shooting percentage
  • Groundballs, forced and unforced turnovers, takeaways, knockdowns, blocks
  • Faceoffs taken and won, faceoff percentage
  • Penalties and penalty minutes
  • Passes attempted, completed, completion percentage, passes received
  • Minutes and quarters played
  • Shot zones — where they shoot from and where on the goal

Goalies

  • Saves, goals against, shots faced, shots on goal faced
  • Save percentage and goals-against average
  • Saves broken out by field zone and goal zone
  • Possessions defended, and cleared possessions defended
  • Opponent goalkeeping calculated automatically from your shooting
11

What you can see during the game

  • Tabs. Actions, Players, Team, Roster, Events.
  • Sortable field-player table, goalie stats, faceoff stats, goals breakdown by side, penalty log.
  • Live shot chart with plotted dots and zone percentages.
  • Game Flow. A possession-by-possession timeline showing each possession’s length, what happened in it, and how it ended — goal, shot, turnover, penalty, failed clear.
  • Event list with quarter and half filters.
12

Second-screen display

Mirror the game to a second iPad for the bench, press box or parents, over a direct local connection with no internet needed.

  • Nine live screens. Scoreboard, field stats, faceoffs, goalies, goals, penalties with live clocks, team stats, shot chart, game flow.
  • Auto-rotate through the screens like a stadium board.
  • A mini scoreboard on every screen, with connection and stale-data indicators.
  • Export the full game report from the display device itself.

iPhone display support is in App Store review for version 1.1.

13

Reports and exports

HTML game report

  • An auto-written recap with headline, game story, key stats and standout players — it recognizes routs, comebacks, nail-biters and defensive battles
  • Scoreboard and team comparison
  • Field, faceoff, goalie and penalty stats
  • Interactive shot chart with a home and away toggle
  • Game flow and the full game log
  • Your team logo
  • Shareable and viewable in any browser

Game CSV

  • Scoring by quarter or half
  • Team summary, plus possession, clear and man-up summaries
  • Player and goalie stats, including opponent goalie stats
  • Team and per-player field and goal shot zones
  • The full timeline
  • Exportable mid-game with one tap from the scoring screen
14

Season rollups

Pick a team, then a season or all seasons, then any subset of games — a full season, a stretch, or the playoffs only.

  • Season summary. Record, win percentage, average goals for and against, goal differential.
  • Team stats as totals and per-game averages, against opponents, across overview, offense, defense and situations.
  • Field-player season table. Twenty-one sortable columns, search, and a totals row.
  • Goalie season cards with save-percentage gauges, per-game averages, a combined view and opponent goalkeeping.
  • Season shot chart heat map, filterable by your team, the opponent, or any single player.
  • Season CSV. Game-by-game results, player season totals including passing, goalie totals with GAA, and shot-zone summaries.
15

Individual player reports

  • One player, or the whole roster in a single document with a page each.
  • Separate layouts for field players and goalies.
  • Exactly the games you choose — a season, a stretch, playoffs only.
  • Print, save as PDF, or share, ready to hand to players, parents or recruiters.
16

Teams, rosters and seasons

  • Multiple teams, each with a name, abbreviation, level, colors and logo.
  • Roster import from a photo. On-device text recognition reads numbers, names and positions.
  • Positions. Attack, midfield, defense, goalie, LSM, FOGO and SSDM.
  • Seasons belong to a team, with a running record on each team card.
  • Per-team defaults. Format, period length, running clock, tracking mode, and home and away jersey colors.
  • Set a default team, and move games between seasons.
17

Getting started

  • A built-in demo game, fully completed, so you can explore every screen before tracking your own.
  • All game data stays on the iPad.
  • Free for three tracked games.
  • Pro, monthly or annual, adds unlimited games plus CSV exports, HTML game reports and player reports.
