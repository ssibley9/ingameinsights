Lacrosse, scored properly.

Track the game as it happens, then watch both teams compared on every metric that matters — possession time, turnovers, clears, shooting — live, while you can still change something.

01

Track

The buttons change with the game, so the next tap is the one you need.

  • Face‑off win rate, live. Every face‑off logged as it happens, with a running win rate for both teams.
  • Ground balls and clears. Tracked as the ball changes hands, attributed to the player who actually won it.
  • Time of possession. Kept for both teams throughout, so the possession story is on screen rather than in your head.
  • Linked actions. One tap records every stat it earns. Log a knockdown, and if your defence recovers the ground ball, that same player is credited with the takeaway. Log a goal, and the last passer is already waiting as the assist.
  • Only what can happen. When the other team has the ball, your clear buttons grey themselves out and the defensive ones arm. You cannot log something the game will not allow.
  • Call it out loud. Voice commands log the fast sequences without you looking down at the iPad.
02

Compare

Both teams, every metric, while you can still do something about it.

  • Side by side, live. Every metric shows your number and theirs on one row, with a bar that fills to match. You read the gap, not the digits.
  • The numbers that decide lacrosse games. Possessions, cleared possessions, possession time, turnovers, shots per cleared possession — alongside goals, assists and shooting percentage.
  • Built for the bench. Mirrored to a second iPad so the coach watches the comparison while the person tracking keeps their head down on input.
  • Honest, not flattering. The better number is highlighted whichever side it belongs to, so a gap you are losing is as obvious as one you are winning.
03

Shots and Saves

Where they came from, and where in the net they went.

  • Two charts, not one. A goal‑mouth grid showing where in the net every shot went, and a field chart showing where it was taken from.
  • Zone by zone on the field. Goals over shots for each area — inside ten left, middle, right, outside ten, beyond fifteen.
  • Misses counted, not discarded. Wide left, wide right, high and blocked are tallied outside the frame, because a shooter who keeps missing left is a coaching problem.
  • Goalie saves in the same picture. Saves sit on the same chart as goals and misses, so you can see what your keeper is actually facing.
  • Filter it down. By team or by a single player.
04

Reports and Season

One game, then twenty.

  • Everything in one report. Face‑off rates, shot locations, ground balls, saves and time of possession together rather than scattered.
  • HTML or CSV. A readable report to hand out, or the raw numbers for your own analysis.
  • Season rollups. Each game folds into season totals and averages without re‑entering anything.
  • Player reports. A page per player — the handout for a parent meeting and the evidence in a playing‑time conversation.
  • Nothing leaves until you send it. Roster and game data stay on the iPad until you share them.
