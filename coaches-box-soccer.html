01

Track

One screen, six jobs, with your thumbs.

  • 1

    BOTH TEAMS, LIVE

    Shots, shots on goal, corners, steals, cards, penalties and pass completion — yours and theirs, side by side, building as the match goes.

  • 2

    WHERE THE BALL IS

    Attacking third, the box, or defensive third. One tap, and possession time starts accruing to that zone — which is how you learn that 62 per cent of possession was not 62 per cent of pressure.

  • 3

    EVERYTHING ELSE THAT HAPPENS

    Shot, corner, foul, offside, penalty, yellow, red, substitution, goalkeeper change. The whole match down one rail, not buried in menus.

  • 4

    WHO HAS IT

    Tap the player on the ball. Numbers greyed out are on the bench, so you cannot credit someone who is not on the pitch.

  • 5

    WHERE THE PASS WENT

    Tap who received it, or mark it incomplete. That chain builds the pass completion figure and hands you the assist when a goal follows.

  • 6

    THEIR SIDE TOO

    The opponent’s numbers sit on the same screen, so one person charts both teams — their steals, fouls and cards included.

02

Two Screens, One Match

Your assistant sees everything. Touches nothing.

  • A free iPhone companion. Connect an iPhone or a second iPad over the same wifi to mirror the match.
  • Everything mirrored live. Scoreboard, live stats, and the field heatmap, updating as you log them.
  • Read–only by design. The bench can follow the match without any chance of disturbing your inputs.
03

After The Whistle

A report your team can actually learn from.

  • HTML or CSV, your choice. A readable report to send, or the raw numbers for your own spreadsheet.
  • Share how you already share. Straight through the iOS share sheet — email, AirDrop, or Messages.
  • Season rollups and player reports. Every match folds into season totals automatically, with a page per player.
  • Nothing leaves until you send it. Your data sits on the iPad until you choose to share it.
