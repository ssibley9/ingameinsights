POSSESSION FIRST

Because the ball is the story.
  • Tap the field. Log where the action actually happens. Possession tracking starts at kickoff and keeps itself.
  • Shots with location and target. Record where the shot came from and which zone of the goal it was aimed at — two dimensions most apps flatten into one.
  • Call it from the touchline. Voice commands let you log without looking down, using voice aliases you set for the names you actually shout.
  • The chart builds as you go. A live shot chart and field heatmap take shape while the match is still running.
  • Lineups and subs. Pick your starting eleven at kickoff and change it as the match goes.
02

TWO SCREENS, ONE MATCH

Your assistant sees everything. Touches nothing.
  • A free iPhone companion. Connect an iPhone or a second iPad over the same wifi to mirror the match.
  • Everything mirrored live. Scoreboard, live stats, and the field heatmap, updating as you log them.
  • Read-only by design. The bench follows the match with no chance of disturbing your inputs.
  • Input from anywhere. The person tracking does not have to stand at the bench — anywhere in range works.
