INSIDE THE TRACKING SCREEN
Coaches Box Lacrosse  ·  One screen, eight jobs
InGameInsights

This is the live input screen, caught at the moment the other team has the ball. The buttons have already changed to match: the defensive actions are armed and the ones that cannot happen have grayed themselves out.

The opponent is carrying — the knockdown and ground-ball grids are live, with 3:12 left in the fourth
1

RUNNING TOTALS

Shots, shots on goal, face-off wins, ground balls, takeaways, knockdowns, clears and possession time — both teams, building as you log.

2

FIELD POSITION

The row turns orange the moment the other team has it. Possession time accrues to them, in the third of the field they are in.

3

ONLY WHAT IS POSSIBLE

With the ball gone, your clear buttons gray themselves out and the defensive ones stay live. The app will not let you log something that cannot happen.

4

KNOCKDOWN

Tap the defender who knocked it loose. If your defense then recovers the ground ball, that same player is credited with the takeaway automatically.

5

GROUNDBALL + POSSESSION

Who picked it up, and whether it turned into possession — in one tap, with the opponent bar beneath for when they win it back.

6

STRENGTH AND SITUATION

Man up, man down or even, and whether the play came off a face-off, a clear or a ride. Every action is stamped with its context, so the reports can tell you your man-up conversion and your clear and ride success rates rather than just your totals.

7

THEIR BALL, LIVE

Pass, incomplete loose ball and incomplete-to-turnover arm themselves while they carry, ready for the next thing that happens.

8

WHO IS ON, AND FOR HOW LONG

Substitute from here and the badge shows how many are on. Because the app knows who was on the field and when, playing time is counted for you rather than estimated after the fact.

Everything above is one screen. No tabs to change, no second app, no paper book running alongside it.
