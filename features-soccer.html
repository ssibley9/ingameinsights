01

Live game tracking

The screen is built around the ball rather than a list of events. The app always knows who has it, so recording what happened next is usually a single tap.

  • Ball-tracking interface. Tap a teammate to record a completed pass and the receiver becomes the new ball carrier, so a full passing sequence is one tap per pass.
  • Every way a pass can end. Completed, incomplete (loose ball, same team), turnover, direct steal, intercepted pass.
  • One-tap actions. Shot, penalty kick, corner, foul, offside, yellow card, red card, substitution, goalkeeper change.
  • Arm, then tap. Select the action, then the player it belongs to.
  • Assist prompt after every goal, for your team and the opponent.
  • Possession changes itself after steals, turnovers, shots and fouls.
  • TEAM attribution when you did not catch which player it was.
  • Both teams on one screen, so one person charts the whole match.
02

Shot tracking

Two taps: where it came from, and where it went. That second tap is the one most apps skip, and it is the difference between knowing a shooter misses and knowing they miss left.

  • Shot origin captured as precise field coordinates, sorted automatically into six field zones plus the penalty spot.
  • Shot target on a nine-zone goal grid, plus miss left, miss right, miss high and blocked.
  • Results. Goal, saved (keeper holds or rebound), miss, blocked.
  • Play context. Open play, corner, free kick, penalty, throw-in, goal kick, counter attack.
  • Corners handled automatically — shots and goals from them are attributed without extra work.
  • Filterable by team and by individual player.
03

Possession and field position

Possession percentage on its own tells you very little. Possession in the attacking third, with the box counted separately, tells you whether you were pressing or passing it round the back.

  • Live possession timer for each team.
  • Zone possession. Offensive third, defensive third, and inside the box, counted separately.
  • Possession count and percentage alongside the raw time.
  • Possession time per player, not just per team.
  • Field possession map comparing both teams’ time in each third.
04

Voice commands

Soccer moves too fast to look down. Call the number and the ball moves to that player; call the color and the number when the other team takes it.

  • Commands for passes (“to 7”), incomplete passes, turnovers, steals, interceptions, possession changes, zone changes, and subs in and out.
  • Possession by shirt color — “blue ball”.
  • Built-in handling for misheard words. “For” becomes 4, “steel” becomes steal.
  • Custom alias editor that teaches the app your accent and your terms.
  • Waits until you finish speaking, so “twenty” does not fire before “twenty-two”.
  • Confirmation banners on screen, and a prompt when a command is not recognized.
05

Game clock and periods

  • Halves or quarters, with preset lengths for varsity, JV, freshman, middle school, club and recreational, or set your own.
  • Stoppage time that counts up after regulation.
  • Clock adjustment in fifteen-second steps, either direction.
  • Overtime and second overtime.
  • Full penalty shootout with round-by-round tracking and its own report.
  • Mark final, and undo it if you marked it early.
06

Substitutions and playing time

The app knows who was on the field and when, so minutes are counted rather than estimated after the fact.

  • On Field and Bench view with tap-to-sub.
  • Minutes played and periods played for every player.
  • Goalkeeper designation that can change at any point in the match.
  • Active-player tracking for the opponent as well as your own side.
07

Goalkeeper stats

Keepers get their own statistics rather than a save count bolted onto a field-player line.

  • Saves, goals against, shots faced, shots on goal faced, save percentage, goals-against average and clean sheets.
  • Penalties faced and penalty goals allowed.
  • Corners faced, with shots and goals allowed from them.
  • Save locations plotted on the goal grid.
  • Part-match keepers handled — a player who keeps for a half and plays out for a half gets both sets of stats.
08

Opponent tracking

Charting both teams from one iPad means your scouting of them is a by-product of tracking your own match.

  • Opponent individual stats by jersey number — goals, assists, shots, steals, turnovers, fouls, cards and more.
  • Opponent shot charts and opponent goalkeeper stats.
  • Add opponent numbers mid-match as you learn them.
09

Corrections and accuracy

Tracking live means getting things wrong occasionally. Every mistake has a way back.

  • Undo the last event with one tap.
  • Delete any event from the log, with the stats reversed automatically.
  • Reassign an event to a different player.
  • Manual stat corrections for players, opponents or team totals.
  • An audit trail of every correction, which appears in the report.
10

Every stat captured

Field players

  • Goals, assists, points
  • Shots, shots on goal, shooting percentage
  • Passes attempted, completed and received, pass percentage
  • Steals, turnovers
  • Corners, plus shots and goals off corners
  • Penalties attempted and made
  • Fouls, offsides, yellow and red cards
  • Minutes, periods played, possession time

Goalkeepers

  • Saves, goals against, shots faced, shots on goal faced
  • Save percentage, goals-against average, clean sheets
  • Penalties faced and allowed
  • Corners faced, shots and goals allowed
  • Save locations on the goal grid

Team

  • Every field-player stat in aggregate
  • Possession time by zone
  • Possession count and percentage
11

What you can see during the match

  • Live scoreboard with team colors, clock, period and possession indicator.
  • Player stats tab with sortable columns and team totals for both sides.
  • Team stats tab with the possession breakdown and field map.
  • Shot chart showing the field and goal views side by side.
  • Game flow timeline and a full event log, filterable by period, player and event type.
12

Second-screen display

A second iPad or iPhone mirrors the match for an assistant, the bench or the press box. It connects device to device, so no venue wifi is required — which matters, because most fields do not have any.

  • Direct device-to-device over Bluetooth or peer-to-peer wifi. No network, no internet.
  • Shows the live scoreboard, possession, team totals, field player and goalkeeper stats, shot charts and game flow.
  • iPhone works as a display-only companion.
  • The display device can generate the full game report on its own.
  • Automatic reconnect, plus a View Last Game option.
13

Reports and exports

Everything leaves through the normal iOS share sheet, so you send it the way you already send things.

Game report

  • Team stats, possession and shot map
  • Shot charts for both teams
  • Field player and goalkeeper tables
  • Opponent stats and the full game flow
  • Stat corrections, with the audit trail
  • Share or print

Individual player reports

  • Season totals and a game-by-game log
  • Season or per-game shot charts
  • Keepers get a shots-faced chart; a player who does both gets both sections
  • One player or the whole roster, one page each
  • Print or save as PDF

CSV

  • Game CSV: summary, possession, player stats, goalkeeper stats, opponent stats, shot coordinates, shootout, full timeline
  • Season CSV: game-by-game results plus season player and goalkeeper stats
14

Teams, rosters and seasons

  • Season management, with games grouped by season or viewed all together.
  • Multiple teams, so JV and varsity live in one app, with a My Teams and Opponents filter.
  • Team colors, fourteen to choose from.
  • Roster import from a photo. Photograph or screenshot a team sheet and on-device text recognition pulls the numbers, names and positions.
  • Eight positions — GK, CB, FB, DM, CM, W, FW, ST.
  • Saved lineups. Starters, defensive, attacking, three sub sets, and a penalty-shootout lineup.
  • Home screen season overview showing games this month and this season.

Roster import, text recognition and everything else here runs on the iPad itself. There is no account to create and nothing is uploaded.
