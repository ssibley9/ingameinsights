01

Draw the Play

Tap a player. Drag. That's a route.

  • Routes with a finger. Tap a player and drag the pencil to draw his route, or tap stops one at a time for exact spots. Arrows, cuts, screens, and stops each draw the way coaches draw them.
  • The ball goes where you say. Drag the ball to a teammate to pass, or to a spot on his route to lead him. The receiver is picked up automatically, ready for the next action.
  • The shot, too. Only the ball handler can shoot — at the end of his route, or catch-and-shoot right where he stands.
  • Defense that follows its man. Lock each defender to his man and he shadows him through the whole play. Show or hide the defense with one tap.
  • Start from a set. Zones and presses come built in — 2-3, 1-3-1, box-and-1, 2-2-1 and more — along with starter plays like Horns Flare and Zipper. Save your own formations as sets, too. Full court or half court, whichever the play needs.
02

Animate in Steps

Teach it the way you'd walk it through.

  • Break it into steps. Entry, flare, shot — each step gets a name and a line of coaching, shown above the court while the play runs.
  • Watch it run. Play, pause, or scrub to any moment. Passes fly, screens set, and the shot arcs to the rim with its own finish.
  • See where everyone came from. Faint tracks from the previous step keep the spacing readable as the play unfolds.
  • Export a video. Landscape for full court, portrait for half court — sized for a team group chat or a film session.
  • Share the play itself. Send a play, a set, or a whole playbook to another coach by AirDrop, Messages, or Mail. Opening a play someone sends you is always free.
03

A Playbook per Team

Varsity, JV, and your summer team — kept apart.

  • Every team, its own playbook. Switch teams from the top of the home screen. Copy a play from varsity to JV, or move a few at once.
  • Rosters in a minute. Type a number and a name and press Return, or import the whole roster from a photo or screenshot. Text recognition runs on the device; nothing is uploaded.
  • Your starters on every play. Set a starting lineup and your players fill the spots in every play automatically. Swap in someone else for a single play with a long press.
  • Numbers your way. Show jersey numbers, initials, or plain 1 through 5.
  • Find it fast. Filter by offense, defense, fast break, out of bounds, press break, or zone, or just search by name.
04

Sideline Whiteboard

For the timeout, when there's no time to build a play.

  • Draw like a dry-erase board. Pick Cut, Pass, or Dribble and the line draws solid, dashed, or zigzag — with the arrowhead added for you.
  • Players you can move. Drag the X's and O's anywhere. Lock the defense to their men and they follow along.
  • Mark the ball. One tap shows who starts with it.
  • Start from a set. Load any saved set, sketch the adjustment, and save the board for later.
  • Best with Apple Pencil. Full court or half court, on the biggest screen on your bench.
