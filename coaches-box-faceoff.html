01

Log

One screen per draw, built for the time between whistles.

  • Won or lost is the only required tap. Everything else is optional, so a busy sequence never leaves you behind.
  • The clamp and the result, separately. Winning the clamp and winning the faceoff are different things. Logging both shows which one your FOGO is losing.
  • Your moves, your names. Clamp, rake, plunger, jump, laser and pinch to start. Add, remove and reorder them to match what your players call things.
  • Where the ball went. Forward left, forward right, back left or back right, from the FOGO’s own view in his stance.
  • Who came up with it. A clean pick, a scrum ground ball, a wing, another teammate, or a violation — for either team.
  • What it turned into. Fast break created or given up, a shot, a goal.
02

Your FOGOs

Track one player or the whole faceoff unit.

  • A badge for each FOGO. Add each player once. Tap one to track him today, and switch mid-game when the coach does.
  • Records that follow the player. Win percentage and clamp percentage for each FOGO across every game and tournament you log.
  • Filter or combine. See one FOGO’s numbers alone, or select several to see the unit together.
  • The other guy, too. Record the opposing FOGO’s number and see how yours does against each one.
03

Totals

The numbers a faceoff coach actually asks for.

  • Win rate when the clamp is won, and when it is lost. Two numbers that tell you whether the problem is the draw or the scrap after it.
  • A direction grid. How many went to each quadrant, and the record on each — an exit he keeps losing shows up fast.
  • Possession, both ways. How your team got the ball and how theirs did, side by side.
  • By move, by quarter, by opponent. Which move is working today, whether he fades late, and who is giving him trouble.
04

Share

A report for the coach, or straight into the stat iPad.

  • A report worth handing over. A clean, branded HTML report for one game, a tournament, or one player. CSV if you want the raw numbers.
  • Works with Coaches Box Lacrosse. At a quarter or half break, AirDrop your faceoff detail to the scorer’s iPad. It matches up with the faceoffs already logged there and fills in the game and player reports. (Syncing needs Coaches Box Lacrosse Pro on the iPad.)
  • Nothing leaves the phone until you send it. No account, no sign-in, no data collected.
